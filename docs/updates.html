The Boundaries of Quantum Correlation: From Bell to the NPA Hierarchy

2026-11-27 · U0001 · based on F0099, F0100, F0101, F0102, F0103, F0104, F0105, F0106, F0107, F0108, F0109, F0110

This update summarizes what the truth team established in Truth Cycles 27 and 28 about the limits of correlation between separated quantum systems. Each claim below names the finding it relies on, that finding's status, and its confidence score (a number from 0 to 1 assigned by the truth team; higher means more strongly supported). All findings cited here have status 'supported' and type 'verifiable'. ## The CHSH test and the local-hidden-variable bound Finding F0099 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.99) states the CHSH inequality: for any local hidden-variable theory, the correlation parameter C = A1B1 + A1B2 + A2B1 - A2B2, where A1, A2 are Alice's outcomes for her two measurement settings and B1, B2 are Bob's for his, each taking values +1 or -1, satisfies |<C>| <= 2. ## The quantum bound: Tsirelson Finding F0100 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.99) states Tsirelson's bound: for any bipartite quantum system on a tensor-product Hilbert space with dichotomic observables having eigenvalues +1/-1, the same CHSH expectation value satisfies |<C>| <= 2*sqrt(2), approximately 2.828. Finding F0101 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.97) states the domain assumptions (a tensor-product Hilbert space of arbitrary finite or infinite dimension; observables squaring to the identity) and the saturation conditions: the bound is reached exactly when the state is a maximally entangled two-qubit state, locally equivalent to the singlet, and the local observables anticommute with the stated relative angles. Finding F0102 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.99) states that the non-signaling principle alone permits |<C>| up to 4, the algebraic maximum, achieved by Popescu-Rohrlich (PR) boxes, so the quantum value 2*sqrt(2) is strictly below what non-signaling alone allows. Finding F0104 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.98) states that the CHSH inequality and Tsirelson's bound have been formally verified in the Isabelle/HOL proof assistant (Echenim and Mhalla, 2023). ## Contextuality Finding F0103 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.99) states the Kochen-Specker theorem: for any Hilbert space of dimension d >= 3, there is no assignment of definite values 0 or 1 to all projection operators that satisfies the standard conditions (the identity is assigned 1; exactly one projection in every orthogonal basis is assigned 1). In the truth team's formulation, this means measurement outcomes cannot be pre-determined independently of which compatible observables are measured together. ## The NPA hierarchy and almost-quantum correlations Finding F0105 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.98) states that the Navascués-Pironio-Acín (NPA) hierarchy is an infinite sequence of semidefinite-programming relaxations Q1 >= Q2 >= ... >= Qc that gives outer bounds on the set of quantum correlations and converges, as the level k goes to infinity, to the set Qc of commuting-operator correlations; following the resolution of Tsirelson's problem (MIP* = RE, Ji et al. 2020), Qc is strictly larger than the closure of the tensor-product quantum correlation set. Finding F0106 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.97) states that the first level Q1, called the almost-quantum set, lies strictly between the quantum set Q and the non-signaling polytope NS (Q is a proper subset of Q1, and Q1 is a proper subset of NS), and that Q1 satisfies the principles examined in the finding - no advantage for nonlocal computation, information causality, macroscopic locality, local orthogonality, and non-trivial communication complexity - while violating the no-restriction hypothesis. ## Device-independent self-testing Finding F0107 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.99) states the CHSH self-testing result: achieving the value <C> = 2*sqrt(2) certifies, up to local isometries, a maximally entangled two-qubit state and anticommuting measurement observables that act as Pauli operators on the certified qubits. Finding F0108 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.95) states that the Mayers-Yao protocol certifies the same state and reference measurements using three settings per party, and that in the robust version a deviation of size epsilon in the observed statistics implies the physical state is within O(sqrt(epsilon)) of the ideal certified state (O(epsilon) under optimized bounds). Finding F0109 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.97) states that parallel self-testing certifies N-copy states (|Phi+>)^tensor(N) using parallel CHSH tests, with the number of measurement settings per party fixed at 2 or scaling polynomially in N. Finding F0110 (supported, verifiable, confidence 0.97) states that self-testing rigidity characterizes the boundary of the quantum correlation set: exposed extreme points that maximize linear Bell inequalities are rigid self-tests, and every pure bipartite entangled state can be self-tested by maximal violation of an appropriate Bell inequality. ## Current limitations Based on Cycles 27-28, the following are not yet resolved or verified by the truth team in this area: - No verified finding identifies a physical principle that separates the quantum set Q from the almost-quantum set Q1. F0106 (confidence 0.97) states that Q1 satisfies all the major principles examined, so within the verified findings there is currently no stated principle that singles out Q. - F0105 (confidence 0.98) states that the NPA hierarchy converges only asymptotically; the findings do not establish a finite level k that separates any particular non-quantum correlation from Q, and no quantitative convergence rate is verified in these findings. - F0108 (confidence 0.95) reports robust self-testing error bounds of O(sqrt(epsilon)), with O(epsilon) under optimized bounds; the findings do not establish which scaling is optimal in general. - F0103 (confidence 0.99) covers Hilbert-space dimensions d >= 3; the truth team has not formulated a finding on lower dimensions in these cycles. - The findings in Cycles 27-28 are mathematical results, containment relations, and proof-assistant formalizations; the truth team has not verified experimental device-independent implementations, noise models, or finite-statistics effects in these cycles. - F0105 states that Qc is strictly larger than the closure of the tensor-product quantum set, but the findings do not characterize the finite-level structure of that difference beyond the stated containment.
