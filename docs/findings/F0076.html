Review note
Claim supported by actual arXiv evidence. Abstracts of O'Connor 2005 (cs/0505034), Paulson 2021 (2104.13792), and Saitou & Noguchi 2026 (2609.13780) confirm these formalizations address Gödel incompleteness via diagonal/fixed-point lemma but do not formalize Tarski's arithmetical truth predicate or undefinability theorem. Confidence 0.88 justified by direct source examination. Refutation condition is precise and testable.

Evidence

arXiv searches verified: (1) O'Connor (arXiv:cs/0505034) proves the Gödel-Rosser incompleteness theorem for a weak axiom system without introducing an arithmetical truth predicate; (2) Paulson (arXiv:2104.13792) mechanizes Gödel's two incompleteness theorems over hereditarily finite (HF) set theory avoiding arithmetical truth semantics; (3) Saitou & Noguchi (arXiv:2609.13780) mechanize Gödel's first and second incompleteness theorems and Solovay's arithmetical completeness theorem of GL in Lean 4 without formalizing Tarski's truth predicate. While the diagonal lemma formalized in these works is the key syntactic machinery for Tarski's theorem, the semantic undefinability theorem itself is not formalized as an internal lemma.
