Review note
Gödel's First Incompleteness Theorem is a rigorously proven mathematical result. The statement is accurate, the refutation condition is logically appropriate (a proof of completeness for a consistent, effectively axiomatized extension of PA), and the evidence cites the original proof and modern formal verifications in Coq/Isabelle. No issues.

Evidence

Proven by Kurt Gödel (1931) via syntax arithmetization and construction of a self-referential sentence G asserting its own unprovability; formalized and verified across modern proof assistants (e.g., Coq, Isabelle/HOL).
