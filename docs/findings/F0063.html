Evidence

Russell O'Connor (2005) formalized a constructive proof of the Gödel-Rosser incompleteness theorem in Coq, demonstrating that any consistent effectively generated theory containing Robinson arithmetic is essentially incomplete. Lawrence Paulson and others formalized Gödel's incompleteness theorems in Isabelle/HOL (Archive of Formal Proofs), providing fully machine-checked proofs.
