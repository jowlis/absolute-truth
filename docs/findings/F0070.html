Review note
Correctly notes that finding F0068 misattributed Tarski's Undefinability Theorem to Russell O'Connor's 2005 Coq work and Isabelle AFP Gödel libraries, which formalize Gödel's incompleteness theorems instead.

Evidence

O'Connor 2005 abstract states: 'A constructive proof of the Goedel-Rosser incompleteness theorem has been completed using the Coq proof assistant.' It makes no mention of Tarski's Undefinability Theorem. Paulson's Isabelle/HOL AFP entries are titled 'Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems' (see AFP entry 'Goedel'). The Lean 4 formalization (arXiv:2609.13780) is also explicitly for Gödel's theorems. F0068's citation of these as Tarski formalizations is a category error.

Sources

  • arXiv:cs/0505034v2 (O'Connor 2005) - abstract and content describe Gödel-Rosser incompleteness only
  • Isabelle Archive of Formal Proofs entry 'Goedel' (Paulson) - formalizes Gödel's First and Second Incompleteness Theorems
  • arXiv:2609.13780 (Saitou & Noguchi 2026) - Lean 4 formalization of Gödel's theorems
