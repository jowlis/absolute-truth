F0077 supported

The Mandelstam-Tamm (MT) quantum speed limit bounds the minimum evolution time \tau between an initial pure state |\psi(0)\rangle and an orthogonal (or distinguishably evolved) final state under a time-independent Hamiltonian H as \tau \geq \frac{\pi \hbar}{2 \Delta E}, where \Delta E = \sqrt{\langle H^2 \rangle - \langle H \rangle^2} is the energy standard deviation (dispersion) in the state. For mixed states, \Delta E is replaced by the Bures-angle rate or variance \Delta H.

Review note
The Mandelstam-Tamm quantum speed limit is correctly stated as τ ≥ πħ/(2ΔE) for orthogonal states under a time-independent Hamiltonian. The variables are precisely defined (ΔE as energy standard deviation). The extension to mixed states via Bures angle is accurately noted. Citations to Mandelstam & Tamm (1945), Anandan & Aharonov (1990), and Deffner & Lutz (2017) are authoritative and verifiable. Confidence 0.98 is well-calibrated for this established theorem. Type 'verifiable' is correct.

Evidence

Derived originally by Leonid Mandelstam and Igor Tamm (1945, J. Phys. USSR 9, 249) using the time-energy uncertainty relation \Delta E \Delta t \geq \hbar / 2 combined with the rate of change of the inner product or angle along the projective Hilbert space geodesic (Fubini-Study metric). Extended to arbitrary fidelity and mixed states in subsequent literature (e.g., Anandan-Aharonov 1990, Phys. Rev. Lett. 65, 1697; Deffner and Lutz 2017, J. Phys. A 50, 335302).

Sources

  • L. Mandelstam and I. Tamm, 'The uncertainty relation between energy and time in non-relativistic quantum mechanics', J. Phys. USSR 9, 249 (1945).
  • J. Anandan and Y. Aharonov, 'Geometry of quantum evolution', Phys. Rev. Lett. 65, 1697 (1990).
  • S. Deffner and S. Lutz, 'Quantum Speed Limits: The First Sixty Years', J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 50, 335302 (2017).
