F0038 supported

Astrophysical quasar absorption-spectrum studies (VLT/UVES and Keck/HIRES) report constraints on spatial variation of α at the level |Δα/α| ≲ few ×10^-5 to ~10^-4 over redshift roughly 0.5 < z < 3.5 (lookback time up to ~11-12 billion years). Some analyses (Webb/King et al.) have claimed evidence for a spatial dipole at marginal significance, but the result is not established and is contested; the robust constraint is an upper bound of order 10^-4.
