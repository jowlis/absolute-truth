F0043 supported

Binary pulsar timing (e.g., the double pulsar PSR J0737-3039) constrains |(1/G) dG/dt| at approximately the ~1×10^-12 to 1×10^-13 yr^-1 level (order 10^-12 yr^-1), under the assumption of general relativity and standard pulsar timing models. This is less tight than LLR but probes a different (strong-field, Galactic) domain and an independent astrophysical system.
