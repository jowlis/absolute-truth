F0041 supported

Optical/microwave atomic and molecular clock comparisons (e.g., SF6, molecular vibrational transitions) and astrophysical molecular observations (ammonia/methanol inversion lines in quasar absorption systems) constrain the fractional time variation of the proton-to-electron mass ratio μ = m_p/m_e to |d(ln μ)/dt| ≲ 1×10^-16 yr^-1 (laboratory) and |Δμ/μ| at the ~10^-5 to ~10^-6 level at redshift z ~ 2-3 (lookback ~10-11 billion years). These are model-dependent constraints depending on molecular sensitivity coefficients.
