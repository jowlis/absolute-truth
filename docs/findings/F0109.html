F0109 supported

Parallel self-testing protocols establish device-independent certification of N-copy tensor product states |Φ⁺⟩^{⊗N} and high-dimensional quantum systems (C^{2^N} ⊗ C^{2^N}) using parallelized Bell tests. Specifically, testing N maximally entangled qubit pairs simultaneously under parallel CHSH inequalities certifies that the shared state is locally isometric to |junk⟩ ⊗ (|Φ⁺⟩)^{⊗N} with a number of measurement choices per party that remains fixed at 2 (independent of N) or scales polynomial in N, enabling efficient high-dimensional quantum hardware verification.

Review note
Correct summary of parallel self-testing: McKague (2016) showed parallel CHSH certifies N EPR pairs with 2 measurements per party (fixed, independent of N); Šupić, Cavalcanti, Bowles (2019) generalized to arbitrary tensor products without increasing measurement choices. The statement about measurement choices scaling constant or polynomial is accurate. Refutation condition is appropriate. Confidence 0.97 is justified.

Evidence

McKague (2016, arXiv:1609.09584) extended parallel self-testing to CHSH for N pairs of qubits; Šupić, Cavalcanti, Bowles (2019, arXiv:1909.12759) generalized parallel self-testing to arbitrary tensor product states using single-copy protocols without increasing measurement choices.
