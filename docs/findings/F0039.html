F0039 supported

Geochemical analysis of the Oklo natural nuclear fission reactor (Gabon, ~2 billion years ago) constrains the change in α over the last ~2×10^9 years to |Δα/α| ≲ 10^-7 to 10^-8 (equivalently |d(ln α)/dt| ≲ few ×10^-17 yr^-1 averaged over ~2 Gyr). Shlyakhter's original argument and subsequent re-analyses indicate α has changed by less than about one part in 10^7 over two billion years.

Evidence

Wikipedia result confirmed Shlyakhter's Oklo finding: 'α has changed less than 10^-17 per year over the last two billion years' (≈|Δα/α| ≲ 10^-7 to 10^-8 over ~2 Gyr). The precise numerical coefficient depends on modeling of neutron-capture resonances (especially Sm-149) and reactor physics, so I treat the bound as approximate and model-dependent; confidence reflects independent confirmation of the general result but uncertainty in the exact coefficient.
