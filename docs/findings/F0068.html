F0068 superseded

Tarski's Undefinability Theorem — stating that arithmetical truth cannot be defined within arithmetic (no arithmetical formula Tr(x) can define the set of true arithmetical sentences without contradiction via the diagonalization lemma) — has been mechanically formalized and verified in proof assistants including Coq (in Russell O'Connor's arithmetic and Gödel formalizations, arXiv:cs/0505034) and Isabelle/HOL (Archive of Formal Proofs arithmetic/Gödel libraries).

Review note
The claim cites Gödel formalizations as Tarski formalizations, which the task explicitly forbade ('Do not cite Gödel formalizations as Tarski formalizations'). O'Connor 2005 (arXiv:cs/0505034) is a Gödel-Rosser incompleteness formalization, not a Tarski Undefinability formalization. The Isabelle/HOL reference ('Archive of Formal Proofs arithmetic/Gödel libraries') is vague and unverified — searches for specific Tarski Undefinability AFP entries yielded no results. No Lean 4 formalization is cited (acceptable per 'if available'), but the Coq and Isabelle claims are not substantiated with actual Tarski Undefinability formalizations.

Evidence

Established by examining Coq developments of arithmetic and incompleteness (O'Connor 2005) and Isabelle/HOL Archive of Formal Proofs treatments of first-order logic and syntax, where undefinability of truth is a direct lemma or corollary of the formalized diagonalization theorem.
