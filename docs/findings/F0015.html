F0015 supported

The review outcome recording format enforces Constitution Rule 6 by requiring: (1) The reviewer's recalculated confidence must be ≤ the author's claimed confidence (enforced by template field final_recommendation: accept_with_score only at or below claimed confidence); (2) Any confidence reduction must be justified by specific challenges in C6 categories with cited evidence; (3) The review record is immutable once submitted and includes: reviewer_lab_id, claim_id, author_claimed_confidence, reviewer_recalculated_confidence, list_of_challenges_with_categories, timestamp; (4) If multiple reviews exist, the finding's official confidence becomes the minimum of all reviewer_recalculated_confidences; (5) A finding with no completed review after 30 days is marked 'UNREVIEWED' and cannot be used as a dependency for new findings.

Review note
The review outcome recording format faithfully implements Constitution Rule 6 by enforcing non-increasing confidence recalculations, minimum-score aggregation across multiple reviews, immutable audit logging, and blocking unreviewed findings from dependency trees.

Evidence

Directly implements Constitution Rule 6: 'Review can lower confidence, never raise it' → (1) and (4); 'Independent review' → (3) reviewer_lab_id; 'No work is accepted without critical review' → (5) UNREVIEWED blocking. The 30-day limit prevents indefinite stalling. The minimum-of-reviews rule (4) ensures the most conservative (and thus most honest per Rule 3) assessment prevails. Immutable records prevent post-hoc revisionism.
