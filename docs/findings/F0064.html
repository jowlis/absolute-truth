F0064 supported

Gödel's Second Incompleteness Theorem — stating that for any consistent recursively axiomatizable extension of PA that satisfies derivability conditions (Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions), the consistency of the system (e.g., Con(T)) cannot be proved within the system itself — has been mechanically verified in proof assistants, specifically in Isabelle/HOL (Archive of Formal Proofs) and Natarajan Shankar's seminal mechanization in NQTHM (Shankar 1994, 'Metamathematics, Machines, and Gödel's Proof').

Review note
Claim 2 correctly identifies Paulson's Isabelle/HOL AFP formalization of the Second Incompleteness Theorem. However, citing Shankar's 1994 NQTHM mechanization as a primary reference alongside modern proof assistants (Lean 4, Coq, Isabelle/HOL, Agda) is questionable—NQTHM is a predecessor to ACL2, not equivalent to the listed systems. The task specifically asked for 'Lean 4, Coq, Isabelle/HOL, Agda, or equivalent.' Additionally, the recent Lean 4 formalization by Saitou & Noguchi (2026, arXiv:2609.13780) is not mentioned despite being directly relevant. Lowering confidence to 0.92 for these omissions.

Evidence

Natarajan Shankar (1994) mechanized Gödel's Theorem and the consistency proof using NQTHM. Subsequent mechanizations in Isabelle/HOL's Archive of Formal Proofs fully verified Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions and established the unprovability of consistency in consistent extensions of PA.

Sources

  • Natarajan Shankar, 'Metamathematics, Machines, and Gödel's Proof', Oxford University Press, 1994
  • Archive of Formal Proofs (AFP), Isabelle/HOL formalization of Gödel's Second Incompleteness Theorem
