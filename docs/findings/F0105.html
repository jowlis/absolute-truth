F0105 supported

The Navascués-Pironio-Acín (NPA) hierarchy is an infinite sequence of semidefinite programming (SDP) relaxations Q₁ ⊇ Q₂ ⊇ ... ⊇ Q_k ⊇ ... ⊇ Q_c that provides outer bounds on the set of quantum correlations. Each level k is defined by the positive semidefinite constraint M⁽ᵏ⁾ ≥ 0 on the moment matrix of products of local measurement operators up to length k. The sequence converges asymptotically (k → ∞) to the set Q_c of quantum commuting correlations (where measurement operators of separate parties commute on a Hilbert space). Following the resolution of Tsirelson's problem (Ji et al., 2020, MIP* = RE), Q_c is strictly larger than the closure of the tensor-product quantum correlation set cl(Q_q), establishing that the NPA hierarchy converges to the commuting-operator quantum correlation set Q_c.

Review note
Correct and precise statement of the NPA hierarchy, its convergence to the commuting-operator quantum correlation set Q_c, and the implication of MIP* = RE (Ji et al. 2020) that Q_c strictly contains the closure of the tensor-product quantum set cl(Q_q). All mathematical definitions, domain boundaries, and refutation conditions are accurate. Confidence 0.98 is justified.

Evidence

Navascués, Pironio, and Acín (2007 PRL 98, 010401; 2008 New J. Phys. 10, 073013) proved that the moment matrix hierarchy M⁽ᵏ⁾ ≥ 0 is complete for commuting measurement operators, yielding convergence to Q_c. Ji et al. (2020, arXiv:2001.04383) proved MIP* = RE, resolving Tsirelson's problem by establishing Q_c ≠ cl(Q_q).
