EUR-QM Applications in QKD Security Frameworks and Quantum Steering: Entropic uncertainty relations with quantum memory provide foundational lower bounds on key extraction rates in Quantum Key Distribution (QKD): (a) In standard BB84 and six-state QKD protocols, EUR-QM bounds secret key generation rates under coherent attacks assuming trusted, characterized source and measurement devices; (b) Device-Independent QKD (DI-QKD) and Source-Device-Independent QKD (SDI-QKD) represent distinct cryptographic frameworks with relaxed device-trust assumptions that also utilize entropic uncertainty bounds as core security steps; (c) Violations of classical entropic uncertainty inequalities in the presence of quantum side information act as quantitative witnesses for quantum entanglement and quantum steering.
