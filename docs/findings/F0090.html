F0090 supported

Purification and Tripartite Formulation: The Berta et al. entropic uncertainty relation is derived by considering a purification of the bipartite state rho_{AB} to a pure tripartite state |psi>_{ABE} on systems A, B, and E (where E is the reference/environment system). By relating the conditional entropy S(X|B) to the entropy S(X|E) on the purifying system, the tradeoff inequality S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) is shown to be equivalent to S(X|B) + S(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c), reflecting the trade-off between the observer's ability to predict measurement outcomes via quantum memory B versus the eavesdropper/reference E's accessible information.

Review note
The conceptual description of the purification argument is correct, but the claim that the bipartite inequality is 'equivalent to' S(X|B) + S(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c) is imprecise. The tripartite relation H(X|B) + H(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c) for a pure state |ψ⟩_{ABE} is used as a lemma to derive the bipartite bound, but the two are not strictly equivalent without additional steps (using chain rules and duality of conditional entropies for the post-measurement states). The evidence also contains a typo ('Church's thesis' instead of 'purification' or 'Schmidt decomposition').

Evidence

Derived in Berta et al. (2010) using Church's thesis / purification of mixed states to a tripartite pure state |psi>_{ABE} and applying Shannon-to-von Neumann entropy bounds across complementary reference systems.

Sources

  • M. Berta et al., Nature Physics 6, 659 (2010); arXiv:0909.0950
