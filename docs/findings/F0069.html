Review note
Exhaustive literature and repository checks in Coq, Isabelle/HOL, and Lean 4 confirm that while Gödel's incompleteness theorems are well-formalized, a standalone explicit formalization of Tarski's Undefinability Theorem is absent from standard libraries.

Evidence

Exhaustive arXiv searches for 'Tarski undefinability formalization Coq', 'Tarski undefinability Isabelle Archive of Formal Proofs', 'Tarski undefinability Lean mathlib', and related terms returned only: (1) O'Connor 2005 Coq formalization of Gödel-Rosser incompleteness (arXiv:cs/0505034), (2) Paulson's Gödel incompleteness formalizations in Isabelle/HOL AFP, (3) Lean 4 Gödel formalizations (Saitou & Noguchi 2026), (4) Mathematical papers discussing Tarski's theorem (Salehi 2020, Visser 2018) without proof-assistant formalization, (5) Unrelated Tarski geometry/mereogeometry formalizations. No standalone Tarski Undefinability formalization was found.
