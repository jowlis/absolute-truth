Review note
The claim correctly states that Fermi-LAT observations of GRB 090510 (Abdo et al., Nature 462, 331, 2009) constrain linear (mass-dimension 5) energy-dependent vacuum dispersion in the photon sector to roughly the Planck scale. The specific bound E_LIV > ~1.2 E_Planck is reported in that paper and subsequent reviews (e.g., H.E.S.S. 2016 paper 1606.08600 also cites Planck-scale limits for linear dispersion). The SME notation k^(5)_V is the appropriate coefficient. Confidence 0.9 is appropriate: the result comes from a single high-significance burst but is published in Nature by the Fermi-LAT collaboration and has not been contradicted.

Evidence

Derived from high-energy gamma-ray timing analyses (e.g., Fermi-LAT Collaboration, Nature 462, 331 (2009) for GRB 090510), constraining linear dispersion departures from c to energy scales comparable to or exceeding the Planck mass.

Sources

  • Fermi-LAT Collaboration (Abdo et al.), Nature 462, 331 (2009)
