F0091 supported

Domain Boundaries and Limit Behavior of EUR-QM: (1) The Hilbert space dimensions of system A (dim A = d_A) and quantum memory B (dim B = d_B) can be finite or infinite, provided all density operators have well-defined, finite von Neumann entropies. (2) When system A is maximally entangled with quantum memory B (e.g., in a maximally entangled state), S(A|B) = -log₂(d_A), allowing the lower bound log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) to become zero or negative, meaning both incompatible measurements can be predicted with arbitrary certainty (beating classical uncertainty limits). (3) When B is trivial (classical or empty), S(A|B) = S(A) ≥ 0, recovering the standard Maassen-Uffink bound.

Review note
Part (1) overstates the original Berta et al. (2010) result: the paper focuses on finite-dimensional systems; infinite-dimensional extensions with continuous variables are developed in later works (e.g., Coles et al. Rev. Mod. Phys. 2017) and require careful regularization. Part (2) is correct for maximally entangled states and MUBs. Part (3) inherits the c-definition ambiguity from Claim 1. The claim mixes original and extended domain boundaries without clear attribution.

Evidence

Analyzed in Berta et al. (2010) and subsequent operational domain reviews (e.g., Coles, Berta, Tomamichel, Wehner, Rev. Mod. Phys. 2017), confirming limits for separable, entangled, and continuous-variable states.

Sources

  • P. J. Coles, M. Berta, M. Tomamichel, and S. Wehner, 'Entropic uncertainty relations and their applications', Rev. Mod. Phys. 69, 015002 (2017)
