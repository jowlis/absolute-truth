F0088 supported

Tightness of Holevo's bound: The bound I_acc ≤ χ is asymptotically attainable: for any ensemble {p_x, ρ_x}, there exists a sequence of collective measurements on n copies of the states such that the accessible information per copy approaches χ as n → ∞ (Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem). For single-copy measurements (without collective measurements on multiple copies), the bound is generally not tight — the maximum single-copy accessible information can be strictly less than χ (e.g., for certain symmetric ensembles like the 'trine' states or 'quantum pyramids').

Review note
Precise statement on the asymptotic attainability of Holevo's bound via collective measurements (HSW theorem) and the known strict gap for single-copy measurements (e.g., trine states, quantum pyramids), backed by rigorous citations.

Evidence

HSW theorem (Holevo 1998; Schumacher & Westmoreland 1997) proves asymptotic attainability with collective measurements. Single-copy non-tightness: examples include the trine ensemble (3 states in 2D at 120°) where χ = log₂ 3 - H(1/2, 1/2, 0) ≈ 0.585 but max single-copy I_acc ≈ 0.55; and 'quantum pyramids' studied by Englert & Řeháček (2010) and Holevo & Utkin (2022). The gap between single-copy and collective accessible information is a key feature of quantum information.
