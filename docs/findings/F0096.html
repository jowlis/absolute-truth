F0096 supported

Domain Boundaries and Limit Behavior of EUR-QM: (1) The original Berta et al. (2010) paper was established for finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces (dim H_A < ∞); extensions to infinite-dimensional continuous-variable (CV) systems were proven later by Furrer, Franz, Berta et al. (2012, Phys. Rev. Lett. 109, 100502) and Tomamichel, Berta, Renes (2013). (2) When system A is maximally entangled with quantum memory B (dim H_A = d_A), S(A|B) = -log₂(d_A); for mutually unbiased bases (c = 1/d_A), the bound log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) becomes zero, allowing complete outcome predictability S(X|B) = S(Z|B) = 0. (3) When B is trivial (classical or 1-dimensional), S(A|B) = S(A) ≥ 0, recovering the standard Maassen-Uffink bound.
