F0082 supported

Domain boundaries and validity conditions for Holevo's bound: (1) The Hilbert space H may be finite-dimensional or separable infinite-dimensional; (2) The ensemble {p_x, ρ_x} may be discrete (finite or countably infinite) with ∑_x p_x = 1; (3) Each ρ_x is a density operator (positive semidefinite, trace 1); (4) The von Neumann entropies S(ρ_x) and S(∑_x p_x ρ_x) must be finite (or the bound is interpreted with extended real values, where S(ρ) = +∞ for states with infinite entropy); (5) The bound holds for arbitrary POVMs {E_y} with ∑_y E_y = I, E_y ≥ 0; (6) For continuous-variable systems with infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, the bound holds when all entropies are finite, but may require regularization (e.g., energy constraints) to avoid trivial infinite bounds.

Review note
Precise and comprehensive domain boundaries and validity conditions for Holevo's bound, covering finite and infinite dimensions, POVMs, and continuous-variable regularization requirements, backed by authoritative sources.

Evidence

Standard quantum information textbooks (Nielsen & Chuang, Wilde, Holevo's own 1998 review 'Quantum coding theorems' and 2012 book 'Quantum Systems, Channels, Information'). The proof uses only the definition of von Neumann entropy, the properties of quantum measurements (POVMs), and strong subadditivity of quantum entropy, which holds for all density operators on separable Hilbert spaces. For continuous-variable systems, see Holevo (1998) and Shirokov (2016) on continuity bounds under energy constraints.
