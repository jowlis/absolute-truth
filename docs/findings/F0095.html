F0095 supported

Purification and Tripartite Lemma Derivation of EUR-QM: The derivation of the Berta et al. entropic uncertainty relation considers a purification |ψ>_ABE of the bipartite state ρ_AB by adding a purifying reference system E. Using the data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy and the duality property of conditional von Neumann entropy S(X|B) = -S(X|E) for pure tripartite states, one derives the tripartite inequality S(X|B) + S(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c) as an intermediate lemma, which directly yields S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B). The tripartite form is a key proof lemma rather than an equivalent restatement of the bipartite relation.

Review note
Accurately describes the purification derivation: purification |ψ⟩_ABE, data-processing inequality, duality S(X|B) = -S(X|E) for pure tripartite states, yielding the tripartite lemma S(X|B) + S(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c) which directly gives the bipartite form. Correctly avoids claiming 'equivalence' between bipartite and tripartite forms. Type=verifiable, confidence 0.96 appropriate.

Evidence

Verified proof structure of Berta et al. (2010) Nature Physics 6, 659–662 (arXiv:0909.0950) and review by Coles et al. (2017) Rev. Mod. Phys. 89, 015002.
