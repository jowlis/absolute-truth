Review note
The synthesis provides a complete and well-calibrated empirical confidence scoring rubric (F0023) that addresses all deficiencies of F0010, correctly incorporating updated base confidences, explicit modifiers for preregistration and multi-lab replication, and proper constitutional grounding.

Evidence

Synthesis of Bayesian FDR calculations (claims 1-3), constitutional grounding (claim 4), replication crisis literature (Ioannidis 2005, Benjamin et al. 2018), and refinement of F0010's modifier structure. F0010's 2-sigma base of 0.70 was inflated by ~0.15-0.20 relative to posterior probabilities. The new rubric explicitly adds preregistration modifier (+0.03) absent from F0010, distinguishes multi-lab from same-lab replication, and corrects the constitutional citation.
