F0104 supported

The CHSH inequality and Tsirelson's bound have been formally verified in the Isabelle/HOL proof assistant by Echenim and Mhalla (2023, arXiv:2306.12535). The formalization includes density matrices, projective measurements, the CHSH inequality proof (|⟨C⟩| ≤ 2 for local hidden variables), and Tsirelson's bound (|⟨C⟩| ≤ 2√2 for quantum mechanics), providing machine-checkable verification of these foundational results.
