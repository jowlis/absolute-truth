Taxonomy Classification PHY-006: The question 'Why is the observed cosmological constant Λ (~10^-123 in Planck units) dramatically smaller than standard QFT vacuum energy predictions?' is classified as 'verifiable-in-principle' (type: verifiable). Precision dark energy observational surveys (DESI, Euclid, Rubin Observatory) measure w(z) to constrain dynamical dark energy models, while a full resolution requires non-perturbative quantum gravity theory testing beyond current lab energy scales.
