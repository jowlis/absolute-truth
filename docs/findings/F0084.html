F0084 supported

Maassen-Uffink relation for mutually unbiased bases (MUBs): If {|a_i⟩} and {|b_j⟩} are mutually unbiased bases in dimension d (i.e., |⟨a_i|b_j⟩|² = 1/d for all i, j), then c = max_{i,j} |⟨a_i|b_j⟩| = 1/√d, and the entropic uncertainty relation becomes H(A) + H(B) ≥ log₂ d. This bound is tight: there exist states (e.g., any eigenstate of A or B) that saturate it.

Review note
Correct application of the Maassen-Uffink relation to mutually unbiased bases (MUBs), yielding H(A) + H(B) ≥ log₂ d with explicit demonstration of tightness for basis states and appropriate citations.

Evidence

Follows directly from the general Maassen-Uffink relation (claim 3) by substituting c = 1/√d for MUBs. MUBs exist in all prime power dimensions d = pⁿ (Wootters & Fields 1989; Ivanovic 1981). Tightness: if |ψ⟩ = |a_k⟩, then H(A) = 0 and H(B) = log₂ d, so H(A)+H(B) = log₂ d. Verified in Ballester & Wehner (2006) and many subsequent works.
