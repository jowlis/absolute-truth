F0086 supported

The Maassen-Uffink bound H(A) + H(B) ≥ -2 log₂ c is not universally optimal: for some pairs of observables, there exist tighter state-dependent lower bounds (e.g., the 'optimal' uncertainty relations of Friedland, Gheorghiu, and Gour 2013; or the majorization-based bounds of Puchała, Rudnicki, and Życzkowski 2013). However, the Maassen-Uffink bound is optimal among all bounds that depend only on the maximum overlap c and not on the full structure of the unitary relating the two bases.

Review note
Accurate contextualization of the non-optimality of the Maassen-Uffink bound across all pairs of observables while correctly noting its optimality within the class of c-dependent bounds, backed by recent literature (Abdelkhalek et al. 2015, Friedland et al. 2013).

Evidence

Abdelkhalek et al. (2015) arXiv:1509.00398 show the Maassen-Uffink bound is non-optimal by characterizing the optimal state-dependent bound. De Vicente & Sánchez-Ruiz (2007) arXiv:0709.1438 give an improved bound for a wide class of observables. Friedland, Gheorghiu, Gour (2013) Phys. Rev. Lett. 111:230401 give a tight bound via majorization. The Maassen-Uffink bound is 'optimal' only in the restricted class of c-dependent bounds (Wehner & Winter 2010).
