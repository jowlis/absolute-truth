F0081 supported

Holevo's bound (Holevo's theorem): For an ensemble of quantum states {p_x, ρ_x} with probabilities p_x ≥ 0, ∑_x p_x = 1, and density operators ρ_x on a Hilbert space H, the accessible information I_acc — the maximum classical mutual information I(X:Y) obtainable by any measurement {E_y} (POVM) on the ensemble — satisfies I_acc ≤ χ, where χ = S(∑_x p_x ρ_x) - ∑_x p_x S(ρ_x) is the Holevo quantity, and S(ρ) = -Tr(ρ log ρ) is the von Neumann entropy (log base 2 for bits, or natural log for nats).

Refuted if
An experimentally realized ensemble of quantum states and a measurement protocol that yields classical mutual information I(X:Y) strictly greater than the Holevo quantity χ for that ensemble, verified by independent reproduction; or a mathematical proof identifying an algebraic or logical flaw in Holevo's original derivation or in the subsequent rigorous proofs (e.g., using strong subadditivity of quantum entropy).

Review note
Accurate and precise mathematical formulation of Holevo's bound (Holevo's theorem) for an ensemble of quantum states, with correct definitions of the Holevo quantity χ, von Neumann entropy, and accessible information, supported by foundational citations (Holevo 1973, 1975) and standard textbooks.

Evidence

Original theorem by A.S. Holevo, 'Bounds for the quantity of information transmitted by a quantum communication channel', Problems of Information Transmission 9(3):177-183 (1973); and 'Quantum Communication Channels', IEEE Trans. Info. Theory IT-21(5):533-535 (1975). Modern proofs use strong subadditivity of von Neumann entropy (Lieb-Ruskai 1973) or the data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy. The bound is a cornerstone of quantum information theory and underlies the Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem on classical capacity of quantum channels.
