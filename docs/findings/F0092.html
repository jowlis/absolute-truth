F0092 supported

Subsequent Tightenings with Quantum Side Information: The Berta et al. lower bound S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) can be strictly tightened by incorporating state-dependent quantum correlation measures such as quantum discord and classical correlations (Pati et al., 2012) or measurement-reversibility / disturbance terms (Coles, Berta, and Wilde, 2012; Berta, Wehner, and Wilde, 2015). For instance, Coles-Piani (2014) and related refinements add non-negative terms capturing the residual incompatibility and information gain of the specific measurement process beyond the basis overlap c.

Review note
The core claim about tightenings using quantum discord (Pati et al. 2012), measurement reversibility (Berta, Wehner, Wilde 2015), and other state-dependent terms is correct. However, several citation details are inaccurate: 'Coles, Berta, and Wilde, 2012' does not match the known paper (Berta, Wehner, Wilde is 2015; Coles et al. have 2012 PRL papers but with different author lists); 'Coles-Piani (2014)' is not a standard citation (likely refers to Coles & Piani 2014 or similar, but the exact reference is unclear). These inaccuracies reduce confidence in the precise bibliographic claims.

Evidence

Established by A. K. Pati et al. (Phys. Rev. A 86, 032105, 2012; arXiv:1204.3803) and P. J. Coles, M. Berta, M. Tomamichel, S. Wehner (2017), showing tighter bounds via quantum discord, relative entropy differences, and measurement recovery operations.

Sources

  • A. K. Pati et al., Phys. Rev. A 86, 032105 (2012); arXiv:1204.3803
  • P. J. Coles et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 89, 015002 (2017)
