F0083 supported

Maassen-Uffink entropic uncertainty relation (general form): For a quantum system in a pure state |ψ⟩ ∈ H (dim H = d < ∞), and two observables A = ∑_i a_i |a_i⟩⟨a_i|, B = ∑_j b_j |b_j⟩⟨b_j| with non-degenerate discrete spectra and eigenbases {|a_i⟩}, {|b_j⟩}, the Shannon entropies of the measurement outcome distributions satisfy H(A) + H(B) ≥ -2 log₂ c, where H(A) = -∑_i |⟨a_i|ψ⟩|² log₂ |⟨a_i|ψ⟩|², H(B) = -∑_j |⟨b_j|ψ⟩|² log₂ |⟨b_j|ψ⟩|², and c = max_{i,j} |⟨a_i|b_j⟩| is the maximum overlap between the two eigenbases. (Log base 2 gives bits; natural log gives nats with factor 1/ln 2 adjustment.)

Review note
Accurate formulation of the general Maassen-Uffink entropic uncertainty relation for pure states with discrete spectra, including correct definition of Shannon entropies, maximum overlap c, and authoritative citation (Maassen & Uffink 1988).

Evidence

Original paper: H. Maassen and J.B.M. Uffink, 'Generalized entropic uncertainty relations', Physical Review Letters 60(12):1103-1106 (1988). The proof uses Riesz-Thorin interpolation theorem and the fact that the unitary transformation between bases has operator norm bounded by c. The relation has been extensively tested and generalized (e.g., to mixed states via purification, to Rényi entropies, to multiple observables).
