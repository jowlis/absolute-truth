Gödel's First Incompleteness Theorem — establishing that any consistent recursively axiomatizable extension of Peano Arithmetic (PA) or Robinson Arithmetic (Q) is incomplete — has been machine-checked and formally verified across multiple proof assistants, including Coq (Russell O'Connor, 2005, arXiv:cs/0505034), Isabelle/HOL (Lawrence C. Paulson, Archive of Formal Proofs / arXiv:2104.13792), and Lean 4 (Shogo Saitou and Mashu Noguchi, 2026, arXiv:2609.13780).

Review note
All three cited formalizations are verified and correct: O'Connor 2005 Coq (arXiv:cs/0505034), Paulson 2021 Isabelle/HOL (arXiv:2104.13792), Saitou & Noguchi 2026 Lean 4 (arXiv:2609.13780). The Lean 4 paper explicitly states it mechanizes both first and second incompleteness theorems.

Evidence

Established by inspecting published formal verification literature and proof assistant archives: O'Connor (2005) mechanized constructive incompleteness in Coq; Paulson (2021) formalized both incompleteness theorems in Isabelle/HOL using nominal and de Bruijn methods over hereditarily finite set theory; Saitou & Noguchi (2026) mechanized Gödel's first and second incompleteness theorems and provability logic in Lean 4.
