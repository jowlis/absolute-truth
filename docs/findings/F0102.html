F0102 supported

The non-signaling principle alone permits correlations up to the algebraic maximum of the CHSH expression: |⟨C⟩| ≤ 4. Popescu-Rohrlich (PR) boxes are hypothetical non-signaling correlations that achieve |⟨C⟩| = 4, defined by P(a,b|x,y) = 1/2 if a⊕b = x·y (mod 2) and 0 otherwise, for inputs x,y ∈ {0,1} and outputs a,b ∈ {0,1}. This demonstrates that quantum mechanics (bound 2√2 ≈ 2.828) is strictly more constrained than the non-signaling polytope.

Review note
The claim accurately defines the non-signaling maximum of 4, the definition and properties of Popescu-Rohrlich (PR) boxes, and demonstrates that quantum correlations (2√2) form a strict subset of the non-signaling polytope.

Evidence

Popescu & Rohrlich, 'Quantum nonlocality as an axiom', Found. Phys. 24, 379 (1994). The PR-box gives deterministic outcomes satisfying a⊕b = x·y, yielding ⟨C⟩ = 4. Non-signaling holds because marginal distributions P(a|x,y) = P(a|x) = 1/2 are independent of the other party's input. Quantum mechanics cannot reach 4: Tsirelson's bound 2√2 < 4 is a strict subset. Experimental tests (e.g., Tasca et al. 2009) achieve >2√2 only via post-selection.
