F0087 supported

Holevo capacity of a quantum channel: For a quantum channel Φ: L(H_A) → L(H_B) (completely positive trace-preserving map), the classical capacity with product state encodings (Holevo capacity) is C(Φ) = max_{p_x, ρ_x} χ({p_x, Φ(ρ_x)}), where the maximum is over all ensembles {p_x, ρ_x} of input states. This is the maximum rate at which classical information can be transmitted reliably through the channel using product-state coding (no entanglement between channel uses). The full classical capacity (with entangled encodings) is the regularization C_∞(Φ) = lim_{n→∞} C(Φ^{⊗n})/n.

Review note
Accurate definition of the Holevo capacity of a quantum channel for product-state encodings and its connection to the regularized classical capacity, referencing the HSW theorem and additivity counterexamples (Hastings 2009).

Evidence

Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem: Holevo (1998) 'The capacity of the quantum channel with general signal states', IEEE Trans. Info. Theory 44(1):269-273; Schumacher & Westmoreland (1997) Phys. Rev. A 56:131. The theorem states C(Φ) = χ(Φ) for product-state codes. Additivity of Holevo capacity (and hence whether regularization is needed) was a major open question; counterexamples to additivity were found by Hastings (2009) using random channels, so C_∞(Φ) > C(Φ) for some channels.

Sources

  • Schumacher, B. and Westmoreland, M.D. (1997). Phys. Rev. A 56:131
  • Hastings, M.B. (2009). Nature Phys. 5:255 (counterexample to additivity)
