F0031 supported

The first-order Peano Arithmetic induction axiom schema states that for any formula φ(x) with free variable x, [φ(0) ∧ ∀n (φ(n) → φ(S(n)))] → ∀n φ(n); in higher-order formal systems (Lean 4, Coq, Isabelle/HOL), this is formalized as the fundamental structural induction principle for natural numbers N.

Evidence

Mechanically formalized as inductive recursors and eliminators in Lean 4 (Init.Prelude: Nat.rec, Nat.recOn), Coq (Coq.Init.Datatypes: nat_ind), and Isabelle/HOL (HOL.Nat: nat_induct). In Calculus of Inductive Constructions (Coq/Lean), Nat is defined as an inductive type with constructors zero and succ, making nat_ind the primitive elimination principle.
