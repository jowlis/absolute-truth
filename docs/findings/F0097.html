F0097 supported

State-Dependent Tightenings of EUR-QM: The Berta et al. lower bound log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) can be strictly tightened by adding state-dependent correlation terms: (a) Pati, Devi, Maccone et al. (2012, Phys. Rev. A 86, 042105 / arXiv:1111.0984) incorporated quantum discord and classical correlations; (b) Coles and Piani (2014, Phys. Rev. Lett. 113, 030402 / arXiv:1402.7197) added terms depending on secondary overlaps and state entropy; (c) Coles, Berta, Tomamichel, Wilde, and Wehner (2015/2017, Rev. Mod. Phys. 89, 015002 / arXiv:1511.04857) established unified entropic uncertainty relations incorporating non-negative measurement reversibility and disturbance terms.
