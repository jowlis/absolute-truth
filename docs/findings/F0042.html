F0042 supported

Lunar Laser Ranging (LLR, including APOLLO) constrains the time variation of Newton's constant to |(1/G) dG/dt| ≲ 1×10^-13 yr^-1 (about 1 part in 10^13 per year) over decades of Earth–Moon ranging, under the assumption of standard general relativity and conventional tidal/Earth-model parameters.

Evidence

Wikipedia result on APOLLO/LLR confirmed lunar ranging to millimeter precision and its use to test gravity, consistent with a strong |Ġ/G| constraint. The precise numerical coefficient (~10^-13 yr^-1) is compiled from standard literature and could not be re-verified against primary sources because arXiv was rate-limited. The bound depends on Earth/Moon tidal and interior models, so it is model-dependent; confidence reflects robust millimeter ranging but uncertainty in the exact coefficient.
