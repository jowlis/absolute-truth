F0094 supported

Corrected Berta et al. (2010) Entropic Uncertainty Relation with Quantum Memory (EUR-QM): For any bipartite quantum state ρ_AB on finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces H_A ⊗ H_B, when Alice measures observable X (eigenbasis {|x_i>}) or Z (eigenbasis {|z_j>}) on system A, the conditional entropies satisfy S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B), where c = max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|² is the squared overlap of the measurement bases. Equivalently, the lower bound is -2 log₂(max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|) + S(A|B). Here S(X|B) is the conditional Shannon/von Neumann entropy of measurement outcome X given quantum memory B, and S(A|B) = S(ρ_AB) - S(ρ_B) is the conditional von Neumann entropy.

Evidence

Verified from Berta et al. (2010) Nature Physics 6, 659–662 (arXiv:0909.0950). In entropic uncertainty relations (Maassen-Uffink, Berta et al.), c is defined as the maximum overlap probability max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|², so log₂(1/c) = -2 log₂ max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|.
