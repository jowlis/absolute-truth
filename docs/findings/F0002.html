Review note
Turing's Halting Problem undecidability is a foundational theorem of computer science. The statement is correct, the refutation condition correctly identifies the diagonalization contradiction, and the evidence references Turing's original paper and standard textbooks. No issues.

Evidence

Proven by Alan Turing (1936) using Cantor's diagonal argument, showing that the existence of such a decider permits constructing an adversarial machine that contradicts the decider's output.

Sources

  • Turing, A. M. (1936). On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem. Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society.
  • Sipser, M. (2012). Introduction to the Theory of Computation (3rd ed.). Cengage Learning.
