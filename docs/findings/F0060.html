CMP-006: The question 'Is BQP (bounded-error quantum polynomial time) contained in the polynomial hierarchy (PH)?' is classified as 'verifiable-in-principle' (type: verifiable). Aaronson (2010) gave an oracle separation showing BQP ⊄ PH relative to an oracle, but the unrelativized question remains open. If BQP ⊄ PH, then quantum computers provide a computational advantage that cannot be captured by any level of the polynomial hierarchy. A proof either way would resolve a major open problem in quantum complexity theory. The question is not verifiable-now (no proof exists) and not purely-conceptual (it has a definite answer in complexity theory).

Evidence

Aaronson (2010) 'BQP and the Polynomial Hierarchy' (STOC 2010) gave oracle separation. Raz & Tal (2019) showed oracle separation for Forrelation. No unrelativized containment or separation is known. BQP ⊆ PSPACE ⊆ EXP is known. The question is a major open problem in quantum complexity theory.
