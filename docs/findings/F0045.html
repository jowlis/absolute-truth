F0045 superseded

The speed of light in vacuum c is exactly 299,792,458 m/s by definition of the metre (SI, since 1983), so its 'variation' is not experimentally meaningful in SI units; dimensionally, any claimed variation of c is a variation of a dimensionless combination such as α or of atomic length/time standards. Lorentz-invariance tests (SME photon/electron coefficients at 10^-18–10^-19, gamma-ray dispersion constraints near the Planck scale) constrain deviations from exact Lorentz symmetry, not a variable c per se.
