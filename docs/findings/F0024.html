Review note
The Bayesian calculation for 3-sigma (alpha = 0.003) is accurate: for prior = 0.05 and power = 0.50, P(H1|sig) = 0.025 / 0.02785 ≈ 0.898; for prior = 0.15 and power = 0.80, P(H1|sig) = 0.12 / 0.12255 ≈ 0.979. This aligns well with Benjamin et al. (2018) and justifies the proposed 0.75–0.80 base confidence range.

Evidence

Bayes' theorem: for α=0.003, prior=0.10, power=0.80: P(H1|sig) = 0.967. For prior=0.05, power=0.50: P(H1|sig) = 0.898. Calculated via run_python. Aligns with Benjamin et al. (2018) proposal of p<0.005 as a more evidentiary threshold (their p=0.005 gives P(H1|sig)=0.947 at prior=0.10, power=0.80).
