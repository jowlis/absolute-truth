Review note
The claim that Lorentz invariance holds to '10^-19 precision level relative to the Planck scale' is ambiguous and potentially misleading. Fermi-LAT GRB 090510 constrains linear Planck-scale dispersion at roughly the Planck mass (E_QG > 1.2 E_Planck), not at a 10^-19 fractional precision. Modern Michelson-Morley optical cavity tests reach ~10^-18 for Lorentz-violating parameters in the photon sector, and Hughes-Drever tests constrain electron-sector parameters to similar levels. The phrasing '10^-19 precision level relative to the Planck scale' conflates different types of bounds. The claim is substantially correct that Lorentz invariance is empirically validated to extraordinary precision, but the specific numeric characterization needs clarification. Lowering confidence to 0.95 pending a more precise formulation.

Evidence

Fermi-LAT observations of GRB 090510 bounding linear Planck-scale dispersion, combined with optical resonator cavity experiments and modern Hughes-Drever tests.
