F0085 supported

Domain boundaries and validity conditions for the Maassen-Uffink relation: (1) The Hilbert space H is finite-dimensional (d < ∞); (2) The state |ψ⟩ is pure (can be extended to mixed states via the concave roof / purification, giving H(A)+H(B) ≥ -2 log₂ c for any ρ, where H(A) = -∑_i ⟨a_i|ρ|a_i⟩ log ⟨a_i|ρ|a_i⟩); (3) Observables A and B have non-degenerate discrete spectra (or more generally, are described by projective measurements with projectors {|a_i⟩⟨a_i|}, {|b_j⟩⟨b_j|}); (4) The overlap c = max_{i,j} |⟨a_i|b_j⟩| is well-defined and < 1 (if c = 1, the bound is trivial: H(A)+H(B) ≥ 0); (5) For infinite-dimensional systems (e.g., position and momentum), the relation holds with c = 1/√(2πħ) for conjugate variables, but requires careful treatment of continuous Shannon entropy (differential entropy) and domain restrictions (e.g., states in the Schwartz space).

Review note
Thorough statement of domain boundaries and validity conditions for the Maassen-Uffink relation, covering finite-dimensional pure/mixed states via purification and infinite-dimensional extensions.

Evidence

Original Maassen-Uffink paper assumes finite dimension and pure states. Extension to mixed states: the map ρ ↦ H(A)+H(B) is concave, so the minimum over pure states in the convex decomposition gives a bound for mixed states (or use purification in a larger system). For infinite dimensions: Białynicki-Birula & Mycielski (1975) for position-momentum; the differential entropy version has subtleties (can be negative). See Coles et al. (2017) Rev. Mod. Phys. 89:015002 for comprehensive review of domain conditions.
