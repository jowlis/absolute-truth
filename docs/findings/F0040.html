F0040 supported

Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) anisotropy observations (e.g. Planck) and Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN) light-abundance constraints limit variations of α at recombination (z ≈ 1100) and during BBN (z ~ 10^9) to roughly |Δα/α| at the percent-to-few-percent level at recombination and tighter (order 10^-2 or better, model-dependent) during BBN. These are indirect, cosmology-model-dependent constraints rather than direct laboratory measurements.
