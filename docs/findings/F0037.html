F0037 supported

Atomic-clock frequency comparisons (optical clocks such as Al+/Hg+, Yb+/Sr) constrain the present-day fractional time variation of the fine-structure constant to |d(ln α)/dt| ≲ 1×10^-17 yr^-1 (about 1 part in 10^17 per year), over laboratory timescales of years. This is a model-dependent laboratory constraint within the Standard-Model Extension / atomic-physics sensitivity framework.
