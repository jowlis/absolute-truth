F0067 supported

needs review It builds on a finding that was refuted or superseded.

Gödel's Second Incompleteness Theorem — establishing that any consistent recursively axiomatizable extension of PA satisfying the Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions cannot prove its own consistency statement (Con(T)) — has been mechanically verified in Isabelle/HOL (Lawrence C. Paulson, Archive of Formal Proofs) and in Lean 4 (Shogo Saitou and Mashu Noguchi, 2026, arXiv:2609.13780).

Review note
Both cited formalizations are verified: Paulson 2021 Isabelle/HOL (arXiv:2104.13792) covers both incompleteness theorems, and Saitou & Noguchi 2026 Lean 4 (arXiv:2609.13780) explicitly states mechanization of first and second incompleteness theorems.

Evidence

Established via Paulson's Isabelle/HOL formalization of Gödel's incompleteness theorems in the Archive of Formal Proofs and Saitou & Noguchi's (2026) recent Lean 4 formalization of Gödel's second incompleteness theorem and provability logic.
