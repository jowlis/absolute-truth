Review note
The claim accurately reflects the literature: Müller et al. (PRL 91, 020401, 2003) and Herrmann et al. (PRD 80, 105011, 2009) are established optical cavity / resonator experiments that constrain dimensionless SME coefficients in the photon and electron sectors at the 10^-18–10^-19 level. The Mattingly (2005) review (gr-qc/0502097) summarizes these bounds. The statement is precise, correctly framed within the SME, and cites the right papers.

Evidence

Established by precision laboratory tests, including rotating optical resonators and microwave/optical cavity experiments (e.g., H. Müller et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 91, 020401 (2003); S. Herrmann et al., Phys. Rev. D 80, 105011 (2009)), which place stringent limits on coefficients for isotropic and anisotropic Lorentz violation.
