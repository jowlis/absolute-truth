New findings

  • F0094 (supported): Corrected Berta et al. (2010) Entropic Uncertainty Relation with Quantum Memory (EUR-QM): For any bipartite quantum state ρ_AB on finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces H_A ⊗ H_B, when Alice measures observable X (eigenbasis {|x_i>}) or Z (eigenbasis {|z_j>}) on system A, the conditional entropies satisfy S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B), where c = max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|² is the squared overlap of the measurement bases. Equivalently, the lower bound is -2 log₂(max_{i,j} |<x_i|z_j>|) + S(A|B). Here S(X|B) is the conditional Shannon/von Neumann entropy of measurement outcome X given quantum memory B, and S(A|B) = S(ρ_AB) - S(ρ_B) is the conditional von Neumann entropy.
  • F0095 (supported): Purification and Tripartite Lemma Derivation of EUR-QM: The derivation of the Berta et al. entropic uncertainty relation considers a purification |ψ>_ABE of the bipartite state ρ_AB by adding a purifying reference system E. Using the data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy and the duality property of conditional von Neumann entropy S(X|B) = -S(X|E) for pure tripartite states, one derives the tripartite inequality S(X|B) + S(Z|E) ≥ log₂(1/c) as an intermediate lemma, which directly yields S(X|B) + S(Z|B) ≥ log₂(1/c) + S(A|B). The tripartite form is a key proof lemma rather than an equivalent restatement of the bipartite relation.
  • F0096 (supported): Domain Boundaries and Limit Behavior of EUR-QM: (1) The original Berta et al. (2010) paper was established for finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces (dim H_A < ∞); extensions to infinite-dimensional continuous-variable (CV) systems were proven later by Furrer, Franz, Berta et al. (2012, Phys. Rev. Lett. 109, 100502) and Tomamichel, Berta, Renes (2013). (2) When system A is maximally entangled with quantum memory B (dim H_A = d_A), S(A|B) = -log₂(d_A); for mutually unbiased bases (c = 1/d_A), the bound log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) becomes zero, allowing complete outcome predictability S(X|B) = S(Z|B) = 0. (3) When B is trivial (classical or 1-dimensional), S(A|B) = S(A) ≥ 0, recovering the standard Maassen-Uffink bound.
  • F0097 (supported): State-Dependent Tightenings of EUR-QM: The Berta et al. lower bound log₂(1/c) + S(A|B) can be strictly tightened by adding state-dependent correlation terms: (a) Pati, Devi, Maccone et al. (2012, Phys. Rev. A 86, 042105 / arXiv:1111.0984) incorporated quantum discord and classical correlations; (b) Coles and Piani (2014, Phys. Rev. Lett. 113, 030402 / arXiv:1402.7197) added terms depending on secondary overlaps and state entropy; (c) Coles, Berta, Tomamichel, Wilde, and Wehner (2015/2017, Rev. Mod. Phys. 89, 015002 / arXiv:1511.04857) established unified entropic uncertainty relations incorporating non-negative measurement reversibility and disturbance terms.
  • F0098 (supported): EUR-QM Applications in QKD Security Frameworks and Quantum Steering: Entropic uncertainty relations with quantum memory provide foundational lower bounds on key extraction rates in Quantum Key Distribution (QKD): (a) In standard BB84 and six-state QKD protocols, EUR-QM bounds secret key generation rates under coherent attacks assuming trusted, characterized source and measurement devices; (b) Device-Independent QKD (DI-QKD) and Source-Device-Independent QKD (SDI-QKD) represent distinct cryptographic frameworks with relaxed device-trust assumptions that also utilize entropic uncertainty bounds as core security steps; (c) Violations of classical entropic uncertainty inequalities in the presence of quantum side information act as quantitative witnesses for quantum entanglement and quantum steering.
